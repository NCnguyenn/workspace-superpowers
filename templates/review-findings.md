# Review Findings

<!-- Reviewer instructions; remove from a delivered findings report. -->
Use this template for an independent review of a specified artifact and revision. The assigned reviewer inspects the actual material and returns findings; the executing role owns corrections and retesting. Do not use review as a substitute for re-opening and verifying the saved artifact. Record the review dimension, reviewer role, inspected coverage, and limits. Include only observed findings; if none are found, state the inspected scope and remaining unchecked areas instead of inventing sample issues.

Use the [shared severity contract](../skills/reviewing-work/SKILL.md#severity-contract): **Critical** blocks final completion (including fabricated or contradicted evidence, invalid proofs, or a materially broken artifact); **Important** must be fixed unless a clear recorded justification allows deferral; **Minor** is a localized defect to fix when feasible within scope; **Suggestion** is optional improvement. A permitted incomplete draft can remain `draft_incomplete` without pretending missing evidence is a pass. A usable finding identifies the exact artifact/revision and location, the observed problem and its evidence or rule, the impact, and an actionable correction. Do not silently rewrite the deliverable or approve your own work.

## Execution Handoff
Reviewers return findings and concrete fixes. Reviewers do NOT rewrite the deliverable. The executing role applies the fixes, rechecks affected content, and records any unresolved findings in the existing checkpoint. For an Important deferral, retain the finding, justification, affected scope/revision, responsible owner, and clearing action; deferral is not a passed check.
