### 2. Rendered / Exported Artifacts
* **Path:** [e.g., `output/thesis.pdf` or `presentation.pdf`]
* **Format:** [e.g., PDF / PNG]

* **Verification Note:** Verified independently from source artifact

* **Path:** [e.g., `assets/figures/`, `references.bib`, `data/summary.csv`]

## Verification Criteria
* **Integrity Checks:** [Format-specific checks required: layout, page breaks, fonts, styles]
